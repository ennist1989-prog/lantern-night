# Pocket Rogue: Competitor Breakdown (Summary)

_Pocket Research, 2026-11-27_

Games reviewed: Rogue Legend: Roguelike RPG (PocketHaven), Rogue Adventure (SharkLab), Rogue Dungeon RPG (Geometric Applications).

## Key findings

- **Rogue Legend** is the only dice-board roguelike with auto-battles among the three games reviewed.
- Players like its loop but call its gacha monetization predatory.
- None of the reviewed games pairs a board run with fair, offline, pay-once pricing. That gap is the clearest opening for Pocket Rogue.
